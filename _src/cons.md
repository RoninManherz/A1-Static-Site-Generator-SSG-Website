    <h2>Negatives</h2>
    <p>
      I have had some issues navigating daily life without a smart phone.
      2FA is possibly the biggest obstacle. Fortunately my phone has 2FA, but
      I require specific apps, like Microsoft 2fa, steam guard, or my school
      ID. Another big issue is the lack of banking apps. I find it extremely
      hard to keep a mental track of my spending when my check ins on the
      real balance are so few and far in between. The phone is also fairly 
      pricey for what it comes with. My phone costed about $350.
    </p>
